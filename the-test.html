The test

Here's the main event. You'll take one job from booking, through a text message, to being paid – playing both yourself (the groomer) and the customer. About 15 minutes.

Before you start, you should have:
  • done the Business setup (services, plus a customer with a real mobile number), and
  • connected Stripe in the Stripe setup.
NO REAL MONEY MOVES AT ANY POINT IN THIS TEST. Even when you pay in step 5, it's Stripe's practice mode. No card is charged and no bank account is touched.

The one thing that is real is the text messages – they'll genuinely arrive on the phone number you gave the app. Only the payment side is pretend.

  1. Make a new booking

    Create a new booking for one of your test customers – use the one with the real mobile number you gave in Business setup. Pick one of the services you added earlier.

  2. Send the booking confirmation text

    Send the booking-confirmation text to the customer from the app. Then check your phone: did it arrive? Have a read of it as if you were the customer – does it make sense? Does it look right?

    Depending on how it's set up, your phone may open a message ready to go – if so, just tap Send.

  3. Mark the job Complete

    Open the job and mark it Complete. This sends the customer a “your pet is ready” message with a payment link in it. Check your phone again – did that one arrive too?

  4. See it waiting for payment

    In the app, open the Payments tab and go to Outstanding. You should see the job you just completed sitting there, waiting to be paid.

  5. Be the customer: pay the link

    Now put your customer hat on. On your phone, tap the payment link in the text you just received. It opens a payment page.

    Reminder: this is a practice payment. No real money moves.

    Pay with this special UK test debit card:

    Card number4000 0082 6000 0000
    ExpiryAny date in the future (e.g. 12/34)
    CVCAny 3 digits (e.g. 123)
    Please use this exact card – not the “4242 4242 4242 4242” one. You may see that 4242 number online as the standard Stripe test card. Please avoid it: Stripe's practice mode treats it as an international card and shows an inflated fee. The UK card above shows the real, accurate fee for a normal UK debit card – which is what we want you to see.

    What about Apple Pay?

    If you'd like to try it, Apple Pay works here too – if you already have a real card in your Wallet, go ahead and pick it. In test mode, Stripe quietly swaps in a practice transaction behind the scenes, so your real card is never charged. It's completely safe.

Payment made – well done! Now let's see what that looks like from your side of the app.
