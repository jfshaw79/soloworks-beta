Payments, invoices and income

You've paid as the customer. Now let's look at it as the business owner: how the payment shows up, what the fees were, and where to check your earnings. About 5 minutes.

Still test mode – no real money moved. Every amount you see here comes from the practice payment you just made.

  1. Check the Payments tab again

    Go back to Payments → Outstanding. The job should be gone from the list, because it's been paid. (It may take a few seconds – try pulling down to refresh.)

  2. Look at the fee breakdown in Invoices

    Open Invoices and tap into the job you just got paid for. You'll see a breakdown of the payment: what Stripe took as its fee, and what you actually received. Have a look – does it make sense? Is anything unclear?

    Remember, this is a practice payment, so these are pretend amounts – but the fee is calculated the same way a real payment would be.

  3. Peek at the Income tab

    Finally, take a look at the Income tab. This is where you can check your earnings over different time periods – handy for seeing how the business is doing. You don't need to do anything here; just have a poke around and see if it makes sense.

That's the whole test – thank you! Nothing real was charged, and you can leave all of this test data in the app or tidy it up, whichever you prefer. Last thing: I'd love to hear what you thought.
