Business setup

First, let's make the app look like your real business: your services and prices, then a few test customers and their pets. About 10 minutes.

Nothing on this page touches Stripe or moves any money. It's just filling in the app.

Part 1: Add a few services

Before we test bookings, let's put in some of your actual services and prices. It makes the rest of the guide feel like your real business rather than made-up data.

  • Add a handful of services to your price list – a few of the things you genuinely offer.
  • Use realistic pricing based on the size of the dog. For example, a full groom might be one price for a small dog, a bit more for a medium one, and more again for a large one.
  • You don't need your whole price list – three or four services is plenty.

Part 2: Add customers and pets

Now add a few test customers, each with their pet(s).

  • Add three or four customers. Made-up names are fine.
  • Make sure at least one customer has more than one pet, so we can check that works nicely.
Important: use a real mobile number for at least one customer. For one of your test customers, enter your own mobile number, or another mobile you have access to. This is how you'll receive the text messages later, seeing exactly what a real customer would see.

Use made-up numbers for the others, so no real customers are ever texted by accident.
All done? You should now have a few services with prices, and a few customers – at least one with more than one pet, and at least one with a real mobile number. Next up: connecting Stripe.
