The walkthrough

Work through this top to bottom. The first three parts are quick setup; part 4 is the real test.

Please read this once: it's all pretend money. Stripe is running in test mode for this beta. Every card, bank detail and identity detail you enter below is a special “practice” value that Stripe provides. Nothing you type is real, nothing is charged, and nothing is paid out to any bank account.

One thing that is real: the text messages. They'll genuinely arrive on the phone number you give the app, so you can see what a customer would see. Only the payment side is pretend.

Setup: Connect Stripe Test mode

This gets your own (practice) Stripe account linked to SoloWorks, so the app can take payments for you. You'll be setting it up from scratch, as it's a separate account from mine.

Do NOT use your real details in this part. Stripe's practice mode only accepts special “magic” values for the identity and bank questions. Your real date of birth, address or bank account won't work – and you don't need them. Just copy the values in the box below exactly as written.

  1. Open the Stripe setup

    In SoloWorks, go to Settings → Stripe Connect → Connect Stripe. This will take you to a Stripe page in your browser to fill in a few details.

  2. Fill in the basics

    Follow the on-screen questions. For anything about your business or personal life that isn't in the box below (your name, business type and so on), you can put in anything sensible – it's a practice account, so nothing is checked against real records.

  3. Use these exact values when Stripe asks

    Date of birth01/01/1901
    Home address, line 1address_full_match
    Type exactly this text. It's not a real address – that's the point.
    Bank account – sort code10-88-00
    Bank account – account number00012345

    The bank account is only for payout set-up. It's a Stripe test account number, so it can't receive anything real.

  4. Finish and return to the app

    When Stripe says you're done, head back to SoloWorks. Under Settings → Stripe Connect it should now show as connected. If it doesn't, or if Stripe asks for something not covered above, make a note of it – that's useful feedback!

Setup: Add a few services

Before we test bookings, let's put in some of your actual services and prices. It makes the rest of the walkthrough feel like your real business rather than made-up data.

  • Add a handful of services to your price list – a few of the things you genuinely offer.
  • Use realistic pricing based on the size of the dog. For example, a full groom might be one price for a small dog, a bit more for a medium one, and more again for a large one.
  • You don't need your whole price list – three or four services is plenty.
These are just prices in the app. Nothing here touches Stripe or moves any money.

Setup: Add customers and pets

Now add a few test customers, each with their pet(s).

  • Add three or four customers. Made-up names are fine.
  • Make sure at least one customer has more than one pet, so we can check that works nicely.
Important: use a real mobile number for at least one customer. For one of your test customers, enter your own mobile number, or your husband's. This is how you'll receive the text messages in the next part, seeing exactly what a real customer would see.

Use made-up numbers for the others, so no real customers are ever texted by accident.

The full test

Here's the main event. You'll take one job from booking, through a text message, to being paid – playing both yourself (the groomer) and the customer.

NO REAL MONEY MOVES AT ANY POINT IN THIS TEST. Even when you pay in step 5, it's Stripe's practice mode. No card is charged and no bank account is touched.

  1. Make a new booking

    Create a new booking for one of your test customers – use the one with your (or your husband's) real mobile number. Pick one of the services you added earlier.

  2. Send the booking confirmation text

    Send the booking-confirmation text to the customer from the app. Then check your phone: did it arrive? Have a read of it as if you were the customer – does it make sense? Does it look right?

    Depending on how it's set up, your phone may open a message ready to go – if so, just tap Send.

  3. Mark the job Complete

    Open the job and mark it Complete. This sends the customer a “your pet is ready” message with a payment link in it. Check your phone again – did that one arrive too?

  4. See it waiting for payment

    In the app, open the Payments tab and go to Outstanding. You should see the job you just completed sitting there, waiting to be paid.

  5. Be the customer: pay the link

    Now put your customer hat on. On your phone, tap the payment link in the text you just received. It opens a payment page.

    Reminder: this is a practice payment. No real money moves.

    Pay with this special UK test debit card:

    Card number4000 0082 6000 0000
    ExpiryAny date in the future (e.g. 12/34)
    CVCAny 3 digits (e.g. 123)
    Please use this exact card – not the “4242 4242 4242 4242” one. You may see that 4242 number online as the standard Stripe test card. Please avoid it: Stripe's practice mode treats it as an international card and shows an inflated fee. The UK card above shows the real, accurate fee for a normal UK debit card – which is what we want you to see.

    What about Apple Pay?

    If you'd like to try it, Apple Pay works here too – if you already have a real card in your Wallet, go ahead and pick it. In test mode, Stripe quietly swaps in a practice transaction behind the scenes, so your real card is never charged. It's completely safe.

  6. Back in the app: it's cleared

    Return to Payments → Outstanding. The job should be gone from the list, because it's been paid. (It may take a few seconds – try pulling down to refresh.)

  7. Look at the fee breakdown

    Open the Invoices section and tap into the job you just got paid for. You'll see a breakdown of the payment: what Stripe took as its fee, and what you actually received. Have a look – does it make sense? Is anything unclear?

    Remember, this is a practice payment, so these are pretend amounts – but the fee is calculated the same way a real payment would be.

  8. Peek at the Income tab

    Finally, take a quick look at the Income tab. This is where you can check your earnings over different time periods – handy for seeing how the business is doing. You don't need to do anything here; just have a poke around and see if it makes sense.

That's the whole test – well done! Nothing real was charged, and you can leave all of this test data in the app or tidy it up, whichever you prefer.
