Stripe setup Test mode

This connects your own (practice) Stripe account to SoloWorks, so the app can take payments for you. You'll set it up from scratch, as it's a separate account from mine. About 10 minutes.

It's all pretend money. Stripe is running in test mode for this beta. Every card, bank detail and identity detail you enter is a special “practice” value that Stripe provides. Nothing is charged, and nothing is paid out to any bank account.
Do NOT use your real details on this page. Stripe's practice mode only accepts special “magic” values for the identity and bank questions. Your real date of birth, address or bank account won't work – and you don't need them. Just copy the values in the box below exactly as written.

  1. Open the Stripe setup

    In SoloWorks, go to Settings → Stripe Connect → Connect Stripe. This will take you to a Stripe page in your browser to fill in a few details.

  2. Fill in the basics

    Follow the on-screen questions. For anything about your business or personal life that isn't in the box below (your name, business type and so on), you can put in anything sensible – it's a practice account, so nothing is checked against real records.

  3. Use these exact values when Stripe asks

    Date of birth01/01/1901
    Home address, line 1address_full_match
    Type exactly this text. It's not a real address – that's the point.
    Bank account – sort code10-88-00
    Bank account – account number00012345

    The bank account is only for payout set-up. It's a Stripe test account number, so it can't receive anything real.

  4. Finish and return to the app

    When Stripe says you're done, head back to SoloWorks. Under Settings → Stripe Connect it should now show as connected. If it doesn't, or if Stripe asks for something not covered above, make a note of it – that's useful feedback!
