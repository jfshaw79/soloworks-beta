Hi Alex – thank you for testing SoloWorks!

Two big new things have landed in the app, and you're the first person to try them. This guide walks you through setting things up, then trying both out, one small step at a time.

1. Text messages

Send a customer a booking confirmation, and a “your pet is ready” message when the job's done – straight from the app.

2. Card payments

Your customer taps a link in the text, pays by card, and the money shows up in the app. You'll see exactly what you'd receive after fees.

No real money moves – at any point. The payments part runs in Stripe's test mode (a practice version). You'll use pretend card numbers and pretend bank details. Nothing is charged, nothing is paid out, and you don't need to enter any real business details.

How this guide works

There are four short pages. Work through them in order – the first two get things ready, and the last two are the real test. It should take about 40 minutes in total, and you can stop and pick it up again whenever you like.

1 Business setup Add a few of your services with prices, and some test customers and pets. About 10 minutes. 2 Stripe setup Connect a practice Stripe account so the app can take (pretend) payments. About 10 minutes. 3 The test Make a booking, send the texts, and pay as if you were the customer. About 15 minutes. 4 Payments, invoices and income See what the payment looks like from your side of the app. About 5 minutes.
