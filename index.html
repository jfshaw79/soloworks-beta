Hi Alex – thank you for testing SoloWorks!

Two big new things have landed in the app, and you're the first person to try them. This guide walks you through setting things up, then trying both out, one small step at a time.

1. Text messages (SMS)

SoloWorks can text your customers at every step of a job, from the moment it's booked right through to payment – not just a booking confirmation. That includes the confirmation itself, the “your pet is ready” message when you mark a job complete, and the payment link that goes with it.

In this test the texts will land on your own phone, so you see exactly what a customer would see.

This is SoloWorks's SMS integration. WhatsApp for Business will be a separate feature that follows at a later date, so there's nothing to test for that yet.

2. Card payments with Stripe

Stripe is a well-known online payments company. SoloWorks uses it so your customers can pay you by debit card or even Apple Pay, just by tapping a link in a text message – no card machine, and no bank details to swap.

The money is paid directly into your own account, and the app keeps track of what's been paid and what's still owed. For this test we use Stripe's practice mode.

No real money moves – at any point. The payments part runs in Stripe's test mode (a practice version). You'll use pretend card numbers and pretend bank details. Nothing is charged, nothing is paid out, and you don't need to enter any real business details.
